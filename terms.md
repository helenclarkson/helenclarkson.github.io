- Preferred methods of communication are **email** or **SMS**.

- I understand that illness happens and that life sometimes throws unexpected events our way. As a guideline, this is what I think is mutually fair in terms of tutoring attendance:

  - If a session is cancelled **after noon on the day of the session**, there will be a **£15 charge**.
  - The viability of the continuation of the service may be in question if there are **repeated cancellations**.
