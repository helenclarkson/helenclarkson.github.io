## Ethos

Providing a service that's accessible to all is important to me, and I hope you see this reflected in my services.

I care about my impact on the environment, which is why I've limited the places I'm willing to drive to. I also aim to be as paper-free as possible in delivering my tutoring.

I give a percentage of my tutoring profits to [Bedford Food Bank](https://bedford.foodbank.org.uk/) and [Emmaus](https://emmaus.org.uk/).
